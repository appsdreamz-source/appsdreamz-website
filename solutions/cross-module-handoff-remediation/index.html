Solutions · Handoffs

Fix what Oracle does not validate: the handoffs between modules

Hiring, talent, pay and statutory reporting are the same record at different moments. Oracle validates each module on its own terms. Nothing validates the handoffs between them, so that is where we design the checks.

Request the Health Check scopeBook a 45-minute diagnostic

The problem

One record, from the contract to the balance sheet

A clean Recruiting configuration and a clean Payroll configuration can still produce a wrong payslip, because the offer terms never reached the employee record correctly.

Each module passes its own tests. The failure lives in what one hands to the next.

Who it is for

Organisations live on several Fusion HCM modules, or about to go live on them together.

What we do

Scope of the work

  • Handoff map across your modules
  • A designed check for every handoff
  • Offer-to-record validation
  • Mobilisation-to-payroll controls
  • Compensation-to-gratuity-base recalculation
  • Payroll-to-statutory-file reconciliation

Deliverables

  • Handoff map
  • Control design per handoff
  • Implemented checks with test evidence

Approach

We trace one record from the employment contract to the balance sheet, and design a check at every point where it changes hands.

Outcomes

  • Fewer retros and corrections
  • Failures caught at the handoff, not in payroll
  • A process that runs inside the system

Questions

Frequently asked

Which handoffs fail most often?

Offer terms into the employee record, mobilisation dates into payroll, allowances into the gratuity base, and pay into the statutory return.

Is this part of the Health Check?

Yes. Offer-to-record and mobilisation-to-payroll are two of its eight inspection areas.
