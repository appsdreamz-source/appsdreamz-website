The problem

Who should buy it, and when

Live customers with recurring exceptions, programmes approaching go-live, organisations whose implementation partner has handed over, and finance teams questioning a provision.

Buy it before an audit, before a restructure or salary review, before adding a country, or when the same exception has appeared three cycles running.

Who it is for

HR, payroll, IT and finance leaders who need an independent, evidence-based reading of a live implementation.

Two scopes

One scope per engagement

Additional scopes are quoted separately, because that is what keeps three weeks from becoming a surface scan.

Payroll and Statutory

Reads three closed cycles line by line, against the contracts.

Talent and Recruiting

Reads the requisition-to-hire flow against how your team actually hires.

Eight inspection areas

  1. 01Payroll

    Gratuity base

    Which allowances the contract makes eligible

  2. 02Payroll

    Leave accrual and end-of-service provision

    Contractual entitlement, reconciled to the balance sheet

  3. 03Handoff

    Mobilisation-to-payroll handoff

    Dates tracked outside onboarding, before they arrive at payroll as a retro

  4. 04Handoff

    Offer-to-record handoff

    Offer terms landing correctly in the employee record

  5. 05Talent

    Recruiting

    Requisition, offer, approval chain

  6. 06Talent

    Onboarding

    Visa, medical, mobilisation dates

  7. 07Talent

    Performance management

    Goals, performance, succession

  8. 08Talent

    Learning

    Catalogue, assignment, compliance evidence

Questions

Frequently asked

Why three weeks?

It is long enough to read three closed cycles line by line against the contracts, and short enough to keep the scope sharp.

Why only one scope per engagement?

Depth. Covering payroll and recruiting in the same three weeks would turn a line-by-line reading into a surface scan.

Do you need access to production?

We work on a read-only copy of production. We never need write access.

What if we do not continue with you?

The report and remediation plan are yours, written so you can act on them with or without us.

How is the fee credited?

In full, against any work you commission from the findings.

Can our implementation partner attend the walkthrough?

Yes. The walkthrough is held with HR, IT and finance in the room, and your partner is welcome.
