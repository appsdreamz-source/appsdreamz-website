Oracle Fusion HCM implementation, designed around the payroll run

Full-suite Fusion HCM programmes from design through go-live, phased where a single date carries too much risk. Payroll and statutory design start in week one, not after Core HR.

The problem

Implementations fail where modules meet

Most programmes are planned module by module and tested the same way. The failures that matter appear at the handoffs, in statutory outputs, and in the first live payroll cycles.

By then, every open defect becomes a payroll correction, and the business starts working around the system it has just paid for.

Who it is for

Organisations moving to Fusion HCM from E-Business Suite, legacy payroll or spreadsheets, in one country or several.

What we do

Scope of the work

  • Design from employment contracts
  • Enterprise structure and payroll relationship model
  • Configuration and fast formulas
  • Data migration with reconciliation per load
  • Integrations that assert volume and totals
  • Test approach including cross-module and parallel runs
  • Cutover planning and go-live readiness
  • Hypercare and post-go-live support

Deliverables

  • Design documents per module
  • Element set mapped to contracts
  • Integration inventory
  • Migration reconciliation reports
  • Test plan and evidence
  • Cutover runbook

Approach

We decide the go-live shape early. Where several countries, payroll and hiring change at once, we phase it, so each go-live carries risk you can see and own.

Outcomes

  • A go-live with known, owned residual risk
  • First payroll cycles that reconcile
  • A configuration your HRIS team can explain

Proof

A GCC property and facilities group of 5,000–10,000 employees had a full-suite programme that stopped converging. We took it live in phases.

Questions

Frequently asked

Do you implement the full suite or individual modules?

Both. We take eight modules live ourselves: Core HR, Payroll, Absence, Time and Labor, Recruiting, Talent, Learning and Integrations. Compensation, Benefits and HR Helpdesk we configure and test inside programmes another firm leads.

Can you work alongside our existing implementation partner?

Yes. Much of our delivery is for other consultancies, under their badge. We can also take a defined workstream, such as payroll and statutory design, inside a programme someone else leads.

How do you decide on a phased go-live?

When one date carries too much risk: several countries, payroll and hiring changing together, or a migration that cannot be reconciled in a single cutover. The Design Review examines the go-live shape specifically.

Which countries have you localised?

Statutory delivery to date, including work with our delivery partners, covers the UAE, Saudi Arabia, India, Malaysia, Singapore, the United Kingdom and the United States. For any other country we tell you on the first call exactly what we have and have not done.
