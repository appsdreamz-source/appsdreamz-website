One record, from the contract to the balance sheet

Hiring, talent, pay and statutory reporting are the same record at different moments. Oracle validates each module on its own terms. Nothing validates the handoffs between them — so that is where we design the checks.

Handoff 01

Offer terms into the employee record. Visa and mobilisation dates tracked outside onboarding arrive at payroll as a retro.

Handoff 02

Allowances into the gratuity base. A restructure in Compensation moves the base and nothing recalculates the provision.

Handoff 03

Pay into the statutory return. Files pass validation, misclassify a component, and the reconciliation finds it weeks later.
