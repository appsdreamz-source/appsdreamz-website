What we do

Five stages of the same practice. Most firms sell one of them; the difficult work is almost always in what one stage hands to the next.

1

Implement

Full-suite Fusion HCM programmes from design through go-live, phased where a single date carries too much risk.

2

Localise

GCC and India statutory design: gratuity bases built from contract terms, WPS and GOSI file structures, leave accrual on contract rather than calendar, India payroll compliance.

3

Integrate

Bank, statutory, finance, assessment and job board interfaces, built to surface a failure the moment it happens rather than weeks later through the data it corrupted.

4

Stabilise

Programmes that stopped converging, payroll running on manual correction, hiring happening outside the system.

Every cycle completes, so nobody calls it broken. Payroll exceptions get worked through on the Thursday before pay date. An offer is approved over email because the chain in the system stalls. A performance cycle closes because HR chased it in a spreadsheet. The workaround becomes the process, and two years later HR, payroll and talent acquisition have absorbed correction work that no one budgeted for and no one reports.

5

Run

Managed support, quarterly release regression testing, and cycle support from a named team. The regression pack we test against is written from your own configuration, not a template.
