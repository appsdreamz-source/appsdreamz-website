The problem

When Fusion HCM looks configured—but payroll, hiring, or compliance still fails.

Every cycle completes, so nobody calls it broken. The cost shows up as correction work, explained variances and a provision that does not hold.

Payroll exceptions and reconciliation gaps

Problem
Exceptions every cycle and retro producing wrong results, worked through manually before pay date.
Business impact
Overtime for the payroll team, corrected payslips, variances finance cannot explain.
What we do
We trace each recurring exception to the element, formula or upstream record that causes it.
Payroll & Statutory Localisation

Statutory and localisation risk

Problem
A gratuity base that leaves out contract-eligible allowances raises no error. The payslip looks right.
Business impact
The end-of-service provision is understated every month until a settlement exposes it.
What we do
We design the element set from the employment contracts and reconcile it to the balance sheet.
GCC Payroll Localisation

Broken cross-module handoffs

Business impact

What we do
We map every handoff and design a check for each one.

Operating model

A specialist operating model for Oracle Fusion HCM.

Five stages of the same practice. Join at any one, including after another firm has finished. Most firms sell one stage; the difficult work is in what one stage hands to the next.

  1. 01

    Implement

    Full-suite programmes from design through go-live, phased where a single date carries too much risk.

    Failure prevented
    A go-live that turns every open defect into a payroll correction
    Outcome
    A controlled go-live with known, owned residual risk
    Implement service

  2. 02

    Localise

    Statutory design for each country: gratuity and end-of-service bases from contract terms, statutory file structures, leave accrual on contract rather than calendar.

    Failure prevented
    Provisions understated without any error raised
    Outcome
    Statutory outputs that reconcile
    Localise service

  3. 03

    Integrate

    Bank, statutory, finance, assessment and job-board interfaces that assert volume and totals before a file leaves the tenant.

    Failure prevented
    A partial post discovered weeks later through the data it corrupted
    Outcome
    Failures surfaced the moment they happen
    Integrate service

  4. 04

    Stabilise

    Root cause and remediation for cycles that complete only through manual correction.

    Failure prevented
    The workaround becoming the process
    Outcome
    Cycles that close without a correction queue
    Stabilise service

  5. 05

    Run

    Managed support, cycle support and quarterly release regression from a pack written from your own configuration.

    Failure prevented
    A quarterly update breaking a fast formula
    Outcome
    Recurring issues retired, not re-logged
    Run service
