Security & data handling

How we handle your tenant and your employees’ data

Payroll data is among the most sensitive data an organisation holds. These are the principles every engagement follows.

Read-only by default

Health Checks run on a read-only copy of production. We do not need write access to diagnose a problem.

Minimum data

We ask for the cycles, contracts and artefacts the scope needs, and no more. For a Health Check that means three closed cycles and contract templates.

Named people only

Access is given to named consultants on the engagement. No shared accounts, no pyramid of unnamed staff.

You own the access

Your team grants, reviews and revokes every account in your tenant. Scope is agreed before work starts.

Procurement

Questionnaires and agreements

Send your security questionnaire, data processing terms or NDA to aneel@appsdreamz.com and we will respond as part of scoping.

Questions from procurement or InfoSec?

Raise them on the first call. We would rather agree the controls before the work than after it.
